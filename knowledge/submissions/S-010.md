## What changed vs the last upload
Lost-edit recovery through a run_command heredoc. Pre-registered at 33c206d BEFORE upload. That is why the FINAL argument always survives and a non-final one never does, and why `run_command`, whose only parameter is also its last, cannot exhibit it. Why #9's ladder was the wrong shape: losses are a two-state process -- fresh slip 3.5-18% per run, then ~90% repeat...
