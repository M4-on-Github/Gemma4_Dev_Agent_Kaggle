## Takeaway
The closest published match to "vote on the next action at every step": N candidate next actions are sampled from the same model and a majority is taken; it helps up to about N = 10 on one benchmark, hurts monotonically on another, and the vote's own agreement statistics tell you when voting is pointless. The agent is gpt-oss-120b in a browser, not a small model in a repository.

## Method
- Per step, sample N actions from the agent, take a majority (plain voting) or hand the candidates to an arbiter LLM (§3, §4). Agent and arbiter are gpt-oss-120b (§3.1, §3.3). Judge for Online-Mind2Web is Qwen3-VL-30B-A3B-Instruct.
- Uncertainty of the step from the vote distribution: entropy H_t and margin Delta_t (Eq. 6, 7, §4.1). CATTS gates the arbiter on U = H_t or U = 1 - Delta_t, threshold grid tau in {0.2, ..., 0.8} (Eq. 9, §4.2).
- Benchmarks: WebArena-Lite 165 tasks, three seeds; Online-Mind2Web 300 tasks, single run; GoBrowse 341 tasks (§3.1, App. M).

## Relevance (mine)
- Gain from voting on per-step actions saturates at N of about 5 to 10 and is not monotone: the paper's own table shows a decline to 15.1% at N = 20 on a second benchmark. N = 16 is on the flat or falling part of both curves.
- About 40% of steps have a unanimous vote at N = 10 for a 120B model choosing among free-form actions. A voted step with unanimity cannot change anything, so the number to read in EXP-039 is how many of the 8 tasks are contested at all.
- The paper's remedy is to spend the extra compute only where the margin is low, which is the same idea as a first-to-ahead-by-k stop rule (lit-2511.09030).
- Environment is a browser: actions are reversible navigation in many cases and each step is scored by a task success check at the end. A repository edit is not reversible by the next step.

## Ideas to test
- (mine) Claim: in V1 on smoke-8, at least 3 of 8 tasks have all 16 voters on one letter (zero vote entropy). The 40% unanimous figure here (App. J) is for 10 samples of free-form actions; an enumerated 4-letter list should be at least as concentrated. If fewer than 3 of 8 are unanimous, the voter diversity is mostly temperature noise.
- (mine) Claim: tasks with a close vote (lead under 3) are where the winner differs from voter 1; D comes entirely from them. Count from the vote texts the trace records.

## Caveats
Web browsing, gpt-oss-120b, N of 1 to 20 sampled at the agent's own step with an arbiter, no shared mutable repository, no wall-clock cap. Appendix N (other model families) not read.
