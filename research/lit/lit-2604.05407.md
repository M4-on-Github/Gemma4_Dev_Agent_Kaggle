Updated 2026-11-05 (was abstract-only; the Qwen3-8B table below is the small-model data for part E). Read through the fetch tool: Table 1, §3, §4.1, §4.2, §4.4.1, Appendix D quotes.

## Takeaway
A structured action space (readCode returns whole syntactic units; editCode applies syntax-validated AST-level transformations) raised pass@1 for GPT-5-nano from 19.6 to 40.4 but did nothing for Qwen3-8B (13.2 to 13.0), although empty patches fell 179 to 138 for the latter.

## Method
- Baselines read whole files or line ranges and edit by string replacement; CODESTRUCT replaces both (§4.2). SWE-Bench Verified, 500 issues (§4.1); CodeAssistBench Verified, 135 tasks.

## Relevance
Part E. For the sub-10B case the interface is not the binding constraint once the model's reasoning is; for our 31B, edit-interface loss (C-004) is a real mechanism but the 30 of 39 right-file-wrong-fix failures are reasoning failures. Our `edit_file` loss plus identical retry (C-004) is the interface class; the single-argument `run_command` heredoc route (C-005) is the declaratively reachable analog. Read empty-patch rate and resolve together, as the Qwen3-8B row warns.

Transfers to our constraints: partly. Custom tools are not available to us.
