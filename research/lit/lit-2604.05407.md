## Takeaway
Empty patches typically follow repeated invalid edits, parse failures or cyclic tool use. A structured action space cut GPT-5-nano's empty patches 233 -> 36 with a +20.8 pp accuracy gain, while Qwen3-8B's empty patches fell 179 -> 138 with no accuracy gain.

## Method
- Replace free-form tool calls with a structured action space; measure empty patches and accuracy (locator not recorded).

## Relevance
Our `edit_file` loss plus identical retry (C-004) is exactly the interface-failure class. The single-argument `run_command` heredoc route (C-005) is the declaratively reachable analog. Warning: on a reasoning-limited model, fewer empty patches may convert to zero extra resolves.
