## Takeaway
One model role-playing the stages of a homogeneous multi-agent workflow in one multi-turn conversation matches the multi-agent execution on HumanEval, MBPP, math and QA, and shares the KV cache between stages. It is evidence for "a pipeline of same-model roles can run as one loop", not for any gain from the roles.

## Relevance
Part D and E: for one fixed model, the structure of a role pipeline does not need separate agents; the cost of splitting is the lost shared context (single-agent input tokens are higher, so the context is carried). One open small model (8B), on HumanEval only.

Transfers to our constraints: partly. No tools, no repo, single-turn tasks; the all-same-model constraint matches ours exactly (the competition forbids mixed models, kg-743063).
