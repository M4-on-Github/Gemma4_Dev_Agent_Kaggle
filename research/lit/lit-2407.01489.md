## Takeaway
Localize -> repair -> validate with no agent loop; samples several candidate diffs, filters syntax errors, selects by regression tests and self-written reproduction tests.

## Relevance
Its pipeline is not expressible in one declarative agent with a shared `/workspace`; its selection signals are the reference for any best-of-N design (C-015) and they cost the time we do not have.
