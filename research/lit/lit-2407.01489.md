## Takeaway
A fixed localize -> repair -> validate pipeline with no agent loop reaches 96/300 (32.00%) on SWE-bench Lite with GPT-4o at $0.70 per issue (Table 1, §5.1). The selection ablation (Table 4) is the cleanest published decomposition of what each selector adds when hidden tests are unavailable: majority vote alone 77, plus regression tests 81, plus model-written reproduction tests 96, against an all-patch oracle of 126.

## Method
- Sampling: temperature 0.8; 10 patches per location set (1 greedy + 9 sampled), four location sets, "40 patches per bug" (§4 Implementation). Diversity comes from temperature and from different localization sets, not from prompt or edit-format variants.
- Reproduction tests: 40 samples (1 greedy + 39) per issue, filtered to those printing "Issue reproduced" on the unpatched repo, normalised, and the most frequent one kept (§3.3.1, §4).
- Selection: filter by regression tests (keep the patches with fewest regression failures), then by the chosen reproduction test, then majority vote over normalised patches (§5.2.3).

## Relevance
Answers part A. Every selector that moved the number needed execution: the regression tests are the repository's own and are available to us, the reproduction test is the model's own and 94/213 (44%) were valid after the gold patch, which is the false-signal rate any same-model reproduction test inherits. Cost of the signal is small in dollars ($0.25) but not in wall-clock: the paper gives no seconds.

Transfers to our constraints: partly. Frontier GPT-4o, 40 patches, no wall-clock cap, a pipeline we cannot express (shared `/workspace`, no custom code). The measured selector gains come from tests that need executions per candidate, which our 4.5 to 8 minute cap cannot pay for more than 2 to 3 candidates.

## Ideas to test
- (mine) Regression tests alone add 4 to a vote (77 -> 81, 5% of oracle gap) while model-written reproduction adds 15; on our model, rate the fraction of reproduction scripts that fail before the edit and pass after on the gold patch (the 44% analogue) from existing dev-48 traces with `analyze_traces.py`; if under 30%, drop any selector that rests on them.

## Caveats
Lite (300 issues), GPT-4o, localization quality is high on Lite. 126 is an oracle, not an achievable selector.
