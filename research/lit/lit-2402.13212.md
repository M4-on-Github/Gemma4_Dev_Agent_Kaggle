## Takeaway
Self-consistency applied to each action of an interactive agent (exact-match majority vote over k sampled actions) gains only 1 to 2 points over greedy on three benchmarks and often finds no majority at all, because free-form actions rarely match exactly. The paper's fix scores actions by token probability, which needs logprobs; our harness exposes none.

## Method
- Each step samples k actions. SC takes the exact-match majority. Soft-SC takes the action with the best aggregate token probability (mean for Bash and ALFWorld, min for WebShop, chosen on dev sets). Adaptive Soft-SC stops sampling once cumulative min-probability passes a threshold.
- Baselines: greedy (k=1), SC, Adaptive-Consistency (AC).
- Models: CodeLlama-34B main; scaling over CodeLlama 7B to 70B; Mistral-7B for ALFWorld in Fig. 1; GPT-3.5-turbo-0613 and GPT-4 scored by smaller open models.
- Sampling: open models temperature 0.7 (Bash) or 0.9 (WebShop, ALFWorld), top-p 0.9, top-k 40, max 100 tokens; "greedy" is run at temperature 0.7 per the extractor. k = 5 and 10 in the main tables; k from 5 to 20 in Fig. 1.

## Relevance (mine)
- Direct evidence that per-step voting over free-form actions is weak: 86% of Bash steps have no majority at k=5, and exact-match SC adds about 1.6, 2.0, 1.8 points over greedy (Bash, WebShop, ALFWorld at k=10). The EXP-039 vote avoids that failure mode by making the answer one of four letters.
- This is the argument for an enumerated list: it converts free-form actions into something exact-match voting can tally. It does not show that a proposer's list contains the right action.
- Logprob scoring is out of reach: `GENERATION_ALLOWED_FIELDS` in `tools/submission_rules.py:52-55` has no logprobs key (temperature, top_p, top_k, max_output_tokens, presence_penalty, frequency_penalty, stop_sequences, response_mime_type, seed, thinking_config). Soft-SC cannot be built from the tree.

## Ideas to test
- (mine) Claim: with the enumerated list, the share of tasks with no strict plurality among 16 voters (a tie for first) is under 25%; if it is higher, the tie rule decides most picks and the vote equals voter 1.

CodeLlama and Mistral of 2023, 100-token actions, tiny test sets (50 WebShop tasks), temperature 0.7 for the "greedy" baseline per the extractor. Not a repository task.
