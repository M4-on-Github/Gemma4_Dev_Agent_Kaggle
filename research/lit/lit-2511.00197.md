## Takeaway
35-40% of failed SWE-bench Verified trajectories never reach the correct files, and those failures are no shorter than successful localizations: wasted effort on wrong paths, not early quitting.

## Method
- Trajectory analysis of failed vs successful runs (locator not recorded).

## Reported results
| Quantity | Value | Setting | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Failed trajectories never reaching the correct file | 35-40% | SWE-bench Verified | summary |

## Relevance
Localization matters, but our recall check (C-012) showed the graph tools lose to grep; the fix is a cheap, specific grep discipline in the prompt. Our E0 traces say the larger loss is post-LOCATE (C-011).

## Ideas to test
- Count tasks whose first `read_file` touches a gold-patched file in Arm R vs RH traces (zero GPU) to see whether the v24 "locate in a few reads" rule reaches the right file more often than our lineage did.

## Caveats
