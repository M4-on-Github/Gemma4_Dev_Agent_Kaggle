## Takeaway
Pass@k rises monotonically with k; majority voting saturates early. Ranks k patches by regression-test passes, then curated reproduction tests, then majority vote, then patch length.

## Relevance
Defines the selection ladder a best-of-N design would need (C-015). Each rung costs test execution time we do not have under the 12 h budget.
