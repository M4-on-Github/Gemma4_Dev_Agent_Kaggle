## Takeaway
A taxonomy of 14 failure modes in 3 categories (system design, inter-agent misalignment, task verification) from traces of 7 multi-agent frameworks on GPT-4-series and Claude models. Verification failures are a measured third of the problem set, and prompt-level fixes help by single digits.

## Relevance
Part C and D: a same-model verifier in a role pipeline is a documented failure mode (superficial checks), not just a hypothetical one. It does not report a false-approval rate for a patch reviewer.

Transfers to our constraints: partly. GPT-4 era, ProgramDev toy tasks and one SWE-Lite system; taxonomy is annotation, not an ablation of architectures.
