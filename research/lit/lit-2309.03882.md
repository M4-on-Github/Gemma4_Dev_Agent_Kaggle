Read through a fetch tool on the ar5iv HTML (characters 0 to 100,000 of 101,227), 2026-11-05; the author list was confirmed from the abs page the same day. The extractor reported the tables below; Figures 3 and 11 to 15 (per-model recall plots) were not read.

## Takeaway
Instruction-following and base LLMs of 7B to 70B pick one of four lettered options with a model-specific preference for certain option letters (mostly a token bias on the ID symbol), so a vote over a lettered list is partly a vote over the model's letter prior. The bias is largest for 7B models and shrinks with size. Evaluated: 20 LLMs on MMLU, ARC-Challenge and CommonsenseQA, with the options given in a fixed answer-by-letter format.

## Method
- Selection bias measured as RStd, the standard deviation of per-option-ID recalls (§2.2).
- Debiasing: removing IDs, cyclic permutation of the option contents (cost x4 on MMLU), and PriDe, which estimates the ID prior from a share alpha of samples and divides it out (cost x1.15 at alpha = 5%) (§3.3, Table 3).

## Relevance (mine)
- Sixteen voters that share one instruction and one option order share one letter prior, so agreement among them is not independent evidence; temperature only perturbs the sampling around that prior. This is the mechanism behind a vote that always returns the same letter (EXP-039 F2) for the wrong reason.
- Of the debiasing routes in this table, the strongest is an order change, not an instruction change: cyclic permutation (+4.9 to +7.9 average accuracy, RStd -8.6 to -11.2). A debiasing instruction does not help (6.1 vs 5.5, Table 2).
- Tie rule: EXP-039 breaks ties toward voter 1, the lowest temperature, which has the least noise to hide a letter prior, so a ties-to-voter-1 rule imports the prior.
- A proposer that lists its best idea first and a voter that likes the first letter both push toward A. If the winner is A in most tasks, the vote has not measured the candidates.

## Ideas to test
- (mine) Claim: in V1, letter A wins at least 60% of the contested tasks regardless of content. Test: count winning letters per task; compare to the share of tasks where A is the proposer's own stated preference. If A wins no more often than the proposer's own top choice, the vote adds nothing beyond the proposer.
- (mine) Per-voter cyclic permutation is out of reach as the paper does it: the order is fixed by the proposer's `output_key` text and the harness has no code step to reorder it; a prompt sentence ("read the candidates from the last") does not change the letter token. Not recommended.

## Caveats
Not agentic, 2023 models, multiple-choice knowledge questions, no options generated by the same model, no temperature sampling (the bias is measured on the model's top answer).
