## Takeaway
A trained 32B outcome verifier lifts OpenHands from 20.6@1 to 29.8@8 and 32.0@16 on Verified; the oracle is 37.8@8 and 42.8@16. This is a trained-verifier ceiling, not a recipe for us. Separately, SFT on 491 teacher trajectories moved Qwen2.5-Coder 7B from 1.8 to 10.6 on Verified and cut its stuck-in-loop rate from 39.6 to 21.0.

## Method
- Base models Qwen-2.5-Coder-Instruct 7B, 14B, 32B; OpenHands CodeActAgent 2.1 scaffold (§4.1, §4.2).
- Verifier trained on trajectories sampled from SWE-Gym with test-suite outcomes (§5.1).
- Budget: "100 interaction turns or the base LM's 32k context" (App. B.2); successful trajectories about 19 turns and 19,000 tokens (§4.2).
- Agent SFT: 491 successful trajectories from gpt-4o-2024-08-06 and claude-3-5-sonnet-20241022 (§4.2, App. B.4); full-parameter fine-tuning with torchtune, lr 1e-4, up to 5 epochs, global batch 8, context 32768 (App. B.2). LoRA (Unsloth, at most 2 epochs) only for the verifier (App. B.2).
- Second scaffold (MoatlessTools): 7B full fine-tune lr 2e-5, 5 epochs; 32B LoRA rank 64, lr 5e-4, 5 epochs; iterative rejection sampling, 30 rollouts per task at temperature 1.0 (App. B.3, §4.3).

## Relevance
Gain versus N: most of the selected gain arrives by k=8. Transfers to our constraints: no, as a recipe (trained verifier, 8 to 16 full trajectories). It supports one design number: a selector that recovers about half the oracle gap is the upper end of what the published literature shows. For the adapter question (D-006): a small amount of teacher-trajectory SFT lowered the loop rate of the 7B, and a self-generated on-policy mix lowered the 32B's score.

## Ideas to test
- (mine) Claim: the union-of-arms ceiling on dev-48 (EXP-034 Q1) times 0.5 is the selector's realistic gain; if union minus best arm is below 8 tasks, expected best-of-N gain is below the 4-task MDE.

## Caveats
Trained verifier, large k, 32k context cap (same as ours) but 100 turns. The loop and empty-patch rows and the on-policy result come from a fetch extraction of v2 and have no section locator beyond "§4.2 extract"; recheck against the paper before quoting.
