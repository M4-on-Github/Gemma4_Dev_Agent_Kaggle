A search summary quoted "about 0.88 to 0.91 at temperature 0.7"; the abstract page does not give 0.7, so the temperature is unverified.

## Takeaway
Three role-prompted copies of one 14B model write chain-of-thought rationales that are nearly the same in embedding space, so a committee of same-model personas is closer to one voter than to three.

## Reported results
| Quantity | Value | Setting | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mean pairwise cosine similarity of rationales | 0.888 (nomic embeddings), 0.908 (mxbai) | 3 Qwen2.5-14B agents with different role prompts, 100 GSM8K questions | abstract page |
| Effective rank | 2.17 of 3.0 | same | abstract page |
| Proposed consensus (DALC) vs self-consistency | 87% vs 84% on GSM8K, 26% lower token cost; run-to-run variance 1 to 3 points; hint sharing contributes more than diversity weighting | | abstract page |

## Relevance (mine)
- Counter-weight to lit-2310.07088: a persona or role instruction alone leaves the rationale similar. For one-letter voters there is no rationale to vary, so this predicts persona-diverse voters will mostly agree, like temperature-diverse ones.
- The gain claimed (3 points on 100 questions) is inside the stated 1 to 3 point variance.

## Ideas to test
- (mine) Claim: two same-prompt voters at temperatures 0.2 and 0.6 return the same letter in at least 90% of tasks; if lower, the list is truly ambiguous to the model.

## Caveats
Abstract only; 100 questions; math; role prompts; single author, not independently replicated.
