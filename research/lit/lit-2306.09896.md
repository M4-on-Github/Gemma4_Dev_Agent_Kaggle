## Takeaway
The clearest code-specific evidence on whether a model reviewing its own output helps: gains from self-repair are "often modest, vary a lot between subsets of the data, and are sometimes not present at all", and the bottleneck is the quality of the feedback, not the repair step.

## Relevance
Part C: a same-model reviewer has the weak link. External signal (test output, stronger or human feedback) is what lifts repair. We have no stronger model, so the only external signal is execution.

Transfers to our constraints: partly. Function-level tasks and old models; no repo-level agent.
