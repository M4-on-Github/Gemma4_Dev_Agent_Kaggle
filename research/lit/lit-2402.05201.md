## Takeaway
Across 9 models and temperatures 0.0 to 1.0, accuracy on multiple-choice problem sets does not change significantly; it falls quickly after 1.0 and the text becomes incoherent at about 1.6. The only small model tested, Llama 2 7B Chat, is unstable and no better than chance. So a spread up to 1.0 is in the safe region for models that work at all, 1.0 to 1.4 is not.

## Method
- 9 models: Claude 3 Opus, Command R+, Gemini 1.0 Pro, Gemini 1.5 Pro (Preview), GPT-3.5 Turbo, GPT-4, Llama 2 7B Chat, Llama 2 70B Chat, Mistral Large (§2.1, Table 1).
- Temperatures: the first GPT-3.5 sweep ran 0.0 to 2.0 in steps of 0.1 and was stopped at 1.6; all other sweeps ran 0.0 to 1.0 (§2.3, Algorithm 1). Small exam 100 questions (10 per problem set), large exam 1,000; each question answered 10 times (§2.2). Kruskal-Wallis tests (§2.5).

## Relevance (mine)
- The EXP-039 spread is 0.2 to 1.4. Using the paper's boundary, voters above 1.0 sit in the degradation region; at 16 voters in equal steps from 0.2 to 1.4 (step 0.08) that is voters 12 to 16, five of 16 (voter 11 is exactly 1.0). For a one-letter reply the damage shows as a wrong letter or a malformed reply, and a malformed vote counts for nothing in the tie rule.
- The paper tests whole-answer accuracy of single samples, not a vote over one token. It does not say how Gemma 4 E4B behaves at 1.2 to 1.4. It is a reason to measure a per-temperature malformed rate, not a reason to believe 1.4 breaks it.
- Llama 2 7B failing on format shows the small-model risk is formatting, which the vote format has to survive.

## Ideas to test
- (mine) Claim: voter replies at temperature >= 1.2 parse as exactly `PICK: [A-D]` at least 5 points less often than replies at <= 0.6 on the E4B. If the difference is under 2 points, 1.4 is safe for this format on this model and the cap is unnecessary.
- (mine) Claim: capping the spread at 1.0 (0.1 to 1.0, equal steps) leaves the share of tasks where the winner differs from voter 1 within 1 task of the 0.2 to 1.4 spread on smoke-8. A zero-GPU re-tally is not possible (it needs the voters rerun); needs a second screen arm, so only worth it if the first arm shows high malformed rates.

## Caveats
Whole-answer accuracy on multiple-choice exams with 2023 and early-2024 models; no E4B or any Gemma; Llama 2 7B is the only small model; top-p and other sampling knobs not varied.
