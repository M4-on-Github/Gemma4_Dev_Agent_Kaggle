## Takeaway
Agents often reach the correct code and then overwrite or thrash it; in 5 cases a mid-trajectory patch matched gold, and an "edit-commit checkpoint" recovered all 5.

## Method
- Trajectory analysis with a checkpoint intervention (locator not recorded).

## Relevance
Supports the v24 protocol's "edit, one check, one repair, submit" over open-ended re-investigation after the first edit (our retired prompt permitted the latter). Mechanism behind C-002's "finishing" reading.
