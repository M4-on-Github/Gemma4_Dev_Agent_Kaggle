## Takeaway
A 32B model (Qwen2.5-Coder 32B) reaches 37.60% on SWE-bench Verified with long chain-of-thought training and 46.00% when external test-time scaling at budget 8 is added. The external verifier is a separate trained ORM plus execution checks, not the same model.

## Method
- Internal TTC: long-CoT SFT with rejection sampling (§2.2 / Table 2). External TTC: a trained process reward model, a trained outcome reward model (DPO on verified patch pairs), and execution-based checks where the agent writes reproduction code (§2.2.1, §2.2.2).
- Strategies compared at budgets 2, 4, 8 on 100 sampled issues: Dev-Search, Exec, ORM_Exec, Voting (§3.4).

## Relevance
Part A/C. Open 32B, same size class as our 31B: external selection adds about 8.4 points over the internal-only model, using trained verifiers. More budget can hurt (Exec drop at 8; hard bucket), which supports capping N low.

Transfers to our constraints: partly. Training for long-CoT and trained reward models are out of reach (no training in the declarative tree); the "more budget can reduce accuracy" caution transfers.
