## Takeaway
Same-model sampling plus similarity voting gives a small, monotone gain on code (HumanEval) for small and mid-size models, with ensemble size 40. For code the vote is by BLEU similarity, not execution, which is the weakest selector in the rows of this review.

## Method
- N samples from one model, then majority vote; for code, "the BLEU score" between all candidate pairs and the sample with the highest cumulative BLEU wins (§3 Voting; App. A.4). Ensemble size up to 40 (§4).
- Models: Llama2-13B, Llama2-70B, GPT-3.5-Turbo (GPT-4 for comparison) (§4).

## Relevance
Part D: same-model sampling-and-voting works on single-function generation; no SWE-style task is evaluated. Gains are largest for weakest models, which is the only small-model-specific signal.

Transfers to our constraints: no for SWE patches (BLEU over multi-file diffs says nothing about correctness, 40 samples), partly as the existence proof for same-model diversity.

## Caveats
HumanEval, N=40, older models, not agentic.
