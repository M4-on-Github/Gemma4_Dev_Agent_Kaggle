## Proposal

| Tweak | Kind | Tree | Change | Evidence |

## Readings (fixed now)

- Kept: mean resolves > 22 + 3 = 25 per draw over two draws. Mean in (24, 25]: a third draw of each arm decides.
- Process endpoints: tasks at the time cap fall by 4 or more per draw and empty patches fall. If they
  fall and resolves do not rise, thinking was buying correctness; try 2048 next.
- Order change for all 31B candidates, made now because a draw costs 1.5 GPU-h and 14 draws remain:
  each candidate runs d1 first; d2 runs only if d1 >= 22. A candidate whose d1 is below 22 is
  recorded as not kept on one draw. This amends R-001's "two draws of each tree" for the 31B only.
